# Community conduct

Everyone participating in this project's issues, discussions and pull requests is
expected to treat others respectfully, regardless of background, identity or experience.
Ask clear questions, welcome newcomers and discuss code and evidence rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks and publication
of another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not post personal recordings
or sensitive metadata, including when reporting a defect.

The maintainer, [@MrQuality](https://github.com/MrQuality), moderates project spaces and
may edit or remove harmful material, close or lock conversations, or block participation.
Responses should reflect the severity and recurrence of the behavior. Maintainer conduct
is subject to the same expectations.

Use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls for sensitive conduct reports;
see [GitHub's reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Do not post private allegations or identifying information in public threads. The project
does not currently offer an independent moderation team or dedicated confidential conduct
inbox. Security vulnerabilities have a separate route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
